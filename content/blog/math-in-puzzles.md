A logic puzzle gives constraints that narrow the possible answers. Keep track of what each clue rules out.

## Use all the clues
A two-digit number has digits that add to 9. Its tens digit is 3 greater than its units digit. Which number is it?

Let the units digit be u. The tens digit is u + 3, so u + (u + 3) = 9. This gives 2u = 6 and u = 3. The tens digit is 6, and the number is 63. Both clues hold: 6 + 3 = 9 and 6 - 3 = 3.

## Check uniqueness
Finding one answer does not always prove that it is the only answer. Explain why your reasoning accounts for every allowed choice. A list or table is useful when there are only a few possibilities.

## Try it
A two-digit number has digits that add to 11. Its tens digit is 1 greater than its units digit. Find it and check both clues.

The number is 65.
