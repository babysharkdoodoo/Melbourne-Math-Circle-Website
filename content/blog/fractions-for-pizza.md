A fraction can describe a portion of a whole divided into equal parts. Equal-sized pieces are essential when comparing slices.

## Read a fraction
If a pizza is cut into 8 equal slices and you take 3, you have 3/8 of the pizza. The denominator, 8, counts the equal parts in the whole. The numerator, 3, counts the parts selected.

## Equivalent fractions
Half a pizza is the same amount as 2 of 4 equal slices or 4 of 8 equal slices. This gives 1/2 = 2/4 = 4/8. Multiplying the numerator and denominator by the same nonzero number keeps the value unchanged.

## Compare portions
For the same-sized pizza, 1/2 is larger than 1/4. Dividing a whole into more equal parts makes each individual part smaller.

## Try it
Draw a circle divided into 8 equal sections. Shade 6. What fraction is shaded, and what fraction is unshaded?

The shaded portion is 6/8 = 3/4. The unshaded portion is 2/8 = 1/4.
