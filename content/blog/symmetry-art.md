A line of reflection symmetry divides a shape into halves that match when folded along that line. The fold maps each point to a corresponding point on the other side.

## Find a line of symmetry
A square has four reflection lines: two through the midpoints of opposite sides and two along its diagonals. A nonsquare rectangle has two, through the midpoints of opposite sides. Its diagonals are not reflection lines.

## Compare reflection and rotation
A shape has rotational symmetry if a turn smaller than one full revolution maps it onto itself. A square matches after a quarter-turn. A nonsquare rectangle matches after a half-turn.

## Try it
Fold a piece of paper in half and cut a small shape from the folded edge. Unfold it and mark the fold. Compare corresponding edges and corners on each side.

Then draw an equilateral triangle. It has three reflection lines and matches itself after a one-third turn.
