Shapes can be described by their sides, angles, and curves. A drawing's size or orientation does not change these defining properties.

## Compare polygons
A triangle has three straight sides. A quadrilateral has four. A rectangle is a quadrilateral with four right angles. A square has four right angles and four equal sides, so every square is also a rectangle.

Rotating a square until a corner points upward still leaves a square. Check its sides and angles rather than relying on how it sits on the page.

## Curved boundaries
A circle consists of points at a fixed distance from a center. That distance is the radius. Its diameter passes through the center and is twice the radius.

## Try it
Draw two different rectangles with perimeter 20 cm. Do they need to have the same area?

A 6 cm by 4 cm rectangle has area 24 square centimeters. An 8 cm by 2 cm rectangle has area 16 square centimeters. Both have perimeter 20 cm, so equal perimeter does not imply equal area.
