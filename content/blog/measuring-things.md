A measurement pairs a number with a unit. The unit tells you what the number represents, such as centimeters of length or minutes of time.

## Choose a unit
Centimeters are useful for a notebook's width. Meters are more convenient for a room's length. Use the same unit when comparing two measurements.

There are 100 centimeters in 1 meter. A length of 1.35 meters is 135 centimeters. Converting units changes the number used to describe a length, but the length itself stays the same.

## Measure consistently
Place the zero mark of a ruler at one end of the object. Read the mark at the other end and record the unit. If your ruler has a gap before zero, starting at its physical edge introduces an error.

## Try it
A rectangle measures 12 cm by 8 cm. Find its perimeter and area.

Its perimeter is 12 + 8 + 12 + 8 = 40 cm. Its area is 12 × 8 = 96 square centimeters. Perimeter measures the boundary; area measures the surface inside it.
