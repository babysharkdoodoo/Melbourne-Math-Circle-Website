Skip counting adds the same amount at each step. It connects repeated addition with multiplication.

## Count equal groups
For 6 groups of 5 objects, count 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30. The total is 6 × 5 = 30.

Counting by 2 lists multiples of 2. Counting by 5 lists multiples of 5. The starting number matters: 1, 3, 5, 7 also increases by 2, but these terms are odd numbers rather than multiples of 2.

## Work backward
Subtracting the same amount reverses the process. From 30, count backward by 5 to get 25, 20, 15, 10, 5, 0. There are six steps, showing that 30 contains six groups of 5.

## Try it
There are 8 boxes with 4 pencils in each box. Count by 4 to find the total. Then find how many boxes are needed for 40 pencils.

Eight boxes hold 32 pencils. Forty pencils need 10 boxes of 4.
